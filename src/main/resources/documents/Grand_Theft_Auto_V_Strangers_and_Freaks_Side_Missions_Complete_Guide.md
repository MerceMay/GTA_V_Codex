# Grand Theft Auto V: Strangers and Freaks Side Missions Complete Guide

## Overview
Strangers and Freaks (S&F) are quirky side missions triggered by purple "?" icons on the map after "Mr. Philips" (Trevor unlock) and "Pulling Favors" (Franklin). There are **58 missions** across ~20 NPC chains, mostly optional but essential for 100% completion, achievements, and unlocks like money scams, vehicles, and skills.

**100% Completion Requirement**: Complete **all 24 Franklin Clinton missions** (specific chains below). Others optional but fun/rewarding (e.g., Epsilon $2.1M).

## Franklin Chains (100% Priority)

### Tonya Wiggins (5 Sequential Towing Missions)
**Unlock**: Davis, after "Nervous Ron". Phone calls trigger each.

| Mission | Brief Walkthrough | Gold Checklist |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| Pulling Favors | Tow car from apartments to garage; lose cops. | Time <5:00; Keep hooked until delivery. |
| Pulling Another Favor | Tow from impound lot; deliver. | Time <5:30; Keep hooked. |
| Pulling Favors Again | Tow construction car; avoid damage. | Time <7:00; Keep hooked. |
| Still Pulling Favors | Tow from beach; long drive. | Time <6:00; Keep hooked. |
| Pulling One Last Favor (Final) | Tow final car; escape. | Time <5:00; Keep hooked. |

**Rewards**: Tonya moves away; 100% progress.

### Hao - Shift Work
**Unlock**: Win street race (yellow icon) in Vinewood Hills.

**Walkthrough**: Race Hao's Infernus; win lap + full race.

**Gold**: Lap <1:20; Race <2:50; <5 collisions.

**Rewards**: None major.
