# Grand Theft Auto V: Random Events and Collectibles Overview

## Overview
Random Events (RE) and Collectibles are key for 100% completion, side content, rewards, and unlocks in GTA V story mode. RE spawn dynamically as blue/red icons; Collectibles are fixed map items (often glowing).

| Category | Total Items/Events | Required for 100% | Key Rewards/Unlocks | Protagonists |
|----------|--------------------|-------------------|---------------------|--------------|
| **Random Events** | 57 (60 in Enhanced/Next-Gen) | Any 14 | Cash ($50-$100k+), skills (+2-5 Driving/Stamina), vehicles (e.g., Duke O'Death, Peyote), heist crew (Taliana, Packie), stocks, achievements (Altruist Acolyte) | All (some exclusive) |
| **Letter Scraps** | 50 | All 50 | Unlocks "A Starlet in Vinewood" (serial killer mission for Franklin); Space Docker vehicle | All (Chop helps Franklin) |
| **Spaceship Parts** | 50 | All 50 | Unlocks Omega's final Strangers and Freaks mission; rewards alien tech lore | Franklin only (via Omega) |
| **Nuclear Waste** | 30 | None | $23,000 each ($690k total) + $250k bonus ($940k total); requires Sonar Collections Dock purchase ($250k) | Any (sub only) |
| **Epsilon Tracts** | 10+ (hidden) | None | Progress Epsilon Program missions; $2.1M donation scam | Michael only |

**General Tips**:
- RE: Unlock after "Pulling Favors" (LS) and "Mr. Philips" (Blaine). Repeatables have cooldowns (2-7 days). Prioritize for crew unlocks (e.g., Crash Rescue → Taliana). Trevor can feed hitchhikers to Altruists (4 needed for shootout).
- Collectibles: Use maps/guides for locations (no in-game tracker). Franklin's Chop finds scraps efficiently.
- 100% Progress: RE (14/57), Scraps (50), Spaceship Parts (50). Buy Sonar Dock post-Merryweather Heist for waste.
- Enhanced Edition Extras: Duel (Duke O'Death), Sea Plane (Dodo), Monkey Mosaic (Blista).

## Collectibles Guide

### Letter Scraps (50 Total)
- **How to Collect**: Glowing white papers on ground/walls/vehicles. Pick up (Chop auto-finds for Franklin).
- **Progress**: Phone notifications assemble murder mystery.
- **Unlock**: All 50 → "A Starlet in Vinewood" (Franklin: investigate 1975 killing, get Space Docker rocket car).
- **Tips**: 21 in Los Santos, 14 Blaine, 15 countryside. Night best for visibility.

### Spaceship Parts (50 Total)
- **How to Collect**: Blue glowing orbs (Franklin only, via Omega after "Far Out").
- **Progress**: Omega analyzes parts for alien tech story.
- **Unlock**: All 50 → Final Omega mission; subtle hints at UFOs/Kraken sub (dock in Paleto Bay).
- **Tips**: Spread across map (23 Los Santos, 27 Blaine). Use Franklin's special for hard reaches.

### Nuclear Waste (30 Total)
- **Prerequisite**: Buy Sonar Collections Dock ($250k, Paleto Bay) after Merryweather Heist.
- **How to Collect**: Dive with Kraken sub (or any); sonar pings barrels underwater.
- **Payout**: $23k per barrel ($690k total) + $250k bonus = $940k.
- **Tips**: 9 near dock, rest coastal/Pacific. Sell immediately after each.

### Epsilon Tracts (Bonus Collectible)
- **How**: 10+ hidden blue pamphlets for Epsilon missions (Michael post-"Seeking the Truth").
- **Rewards**: Progress scam storyline; donate $2.1M (reclaim via final mission).
- **Tips**: Buy collectible map from Epsilon site ($25k investment).

## 100% Completion Checklist
- **Random Events**: 14/57 (mix repeatables + key unlocks).
- **Collectibles**: 50 Scraps + 50 Parts (triggers Strangers missions).
- **Related**: Buy Sonar Dock for waste (optional cash). Stunts (25/50 jumps, 25/50 bridges, 8/15 knife flights) separate but map-overlap.

**Max Efficiency**: Free roam post-story; use Franklin for scraps/parts. RE cooldowns: Sleep/advance days. Total time: 5-10 hours with map.
