# Grand Theft Auto V: Endings, Choices, and Outcomes

## Overview
GTA V's story culminates in **The Big Score** heist, after which Franklin receives a phone call from Lamar Davis (post-"Lamar Davis" mission). This triggers a critical choice screen with **three endings**:

| Ending | Name                  | Trigger Choice                  | Survivors              | Total Payout (Post-Big Score Split) | Canon? | Gold Medal Time |
|--------|-----------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------|-------------------------------------|--------|-----------------|
| **A**  | Something Sensible   | Kill Trevor (Devin Weston's option) | Franklin, Michael     | Franklin: ~$20M<br>Michael: ~$20M  | No    | N/A            |
| **B**  | The Time's Come      | Kill Michael (Steve Haines' option) | Franklin, Trevor      | Franklin: ~$25M<br>Trevor: ~$25M  | No    | N/A            |
| **C**  | The Third Way (Deathwish) | Save Both (Lester's option)    | Franklin, Michael, Trevor | ~$40M each (recommended)          | Yes   | 21:30          |

**Key Notes**:
- **Save Before Choosing**: Outcomes are permanent per save file (replay via Pause > Missions > The Third Way).
- **Recommendation**: Always pick **C** for max money, all protagonists alive (unlocks full post-game: properties, Lester's Assassinations for billions, 100% completion).
- **Payouts**: Based on Obvious Big Score (~$41M/person pre-ending). A/B reduce shares due to deaths.
- **Post-Story Unlocks**: All endings allow remaining protagonists' content (e.g., Epsilon, properties). Ending C = full access.
- **Achievements/Trophies**: "To Live and Die in LS" (C Gold), "The Moment of Truth" (any ending).
- **Replayability**: Endings replayable post-story; stocks/assassinations unaffected.

## Critical Pre-Ending Choices
- **Big Score Approach**: Obvious > Subtle for max gold (~$201M gross).
- **Crew Choices**: Cheap/experienced crew (Taliana, Daryl, Rickie) minimizes cuts/losses.
- **Lester's Assassinations**: Do Hotel first (mandatory); save others for post-Big Score (invest ~$40M/person → ~$2.1B total).
- **Story Branches**: No major prior choices alter endings (e.g., romances, yoga don't impact).

## Ending A: Something Sensible (Kill Trevor)
**Trigger**: Select "C" (Devin's plan) on Franklin's choice screen.

### Walkthrough
1. Drive to oil fields (Grapeseed).
2. Chase Trevor in dirt bikes; shoot tires/use Franklin slow-mo.
3. Final confrontation: Push Trevor off edge or shoot (headshot for style).
4. Call Lester; return to safehouse.

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
- N/A (no Gold available).

**Outcomes & Consequences**:
| Aspect       | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Survivors**| Franklin & Michael alive; Trevor dead. |
| **Payout**   | Franklin: $20,079,864<br>Michael: $20,804,294 (from Big Score). |
| **Story**    | Devin satisfied; Michael visits Franklin (awkward). No Trevor content. |
| **Post-Game**| Franklin/Michael only: Properties, races, Epsilon (Michael), Strangers (Franklin). No Trevor rampages/bounties. |
| **Achievements** | "The Moment of Truth". |

**Tips**: Easy bike chase; explosives optional. Quickest ending (~5 min).

## Ending B: The Time's Come (Kill Michael)
**Trigger**: Select "B" (Haines' plan) on Franklin's choice screen.

### Walkthrough
1. Drive to Galileo Observatory.
2. Chase Michael in SUVs; shootout on roads.
3. Final standoff: Michael begs; shoot him (multiple shots if needed).
4. Trevor calls (happy); escape Merryweather helos.
5. Return home.

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
- N/A (no Gold available).

**Outcomes & Consequences**:
| Aspect       | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Survivors**| Franklin & Trevor alive; Michael dead. |
| **Payout**   | Franklin: $25,126,273<br>Trevor: $25,145,900. |
| **Story**    | Trevor approves; Franklin uneasy. No Michael content. |
| **Post-Game**| Franklin/Trevor only: Strip club (Trevor), bounties, but miss Michael's yoga/Epsilon ($2.1M scam). |
| **Achievements** | "The Moment of Truth". |

**Tips**: Use slow-mo for chase; helos easy with RPGs. Trevor gets Michael's share vibe.

## Ending C: The Third Way (Deathwish - Recommended)
**Trigger**: Select "A" (Lester's plan) on Franklin's choice screen.

### Walkthrough
1. **Planning**: Meet Lester at church; plan hits on Cheng, Stretch, Devin, Haines.
2. **Assassinations**:
  - **Cheng**: Ambush Triad convoy (Franklin bike sniper).
  - **Stretch**: Kill at car meet (stealth/melee preferred).
  - **Devin Weston**: Strap him in car; push off cliff.
  - **Steve Haines**: Execute on TV show set (post-"Meltdown").
3. Massive FIB/ Merryweather shootout at foundry.
4. Helo escape (Trevor); lose 4-stars.

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
| Req.              | Target |
|-------------------|--------|
| **Headshots**     | 78+ total |
| **Time**          | <21:00 |
| **Accuracy**      | 80%+   |
| **Switch Chars**  | 10x+   |
| **Unmarked**      | No civilian damage |

**Outcomes & Consequences**:
| Aspect       | Details |
|--------------|---------|
| **Survivors**| All three alive & friendly. |
| **Payout**   | Franklin: $40,721,179<br>Michael: $40,688,749<br>Trevor: $40,610,944. |
| **Story**    | Happy ending: Family reunites; trio escapes to freedom. Lamar promotes Franklin. |
| **Post-Game**| Full access: All protagonists, properties, flightschool, races, Lester's remaining Assassinations (Bus, Vice, Multi-Target, Construction → billions), 100% completion. |
| **Achievements** | "The Third Way", "To Live and Die in LS" (Gold), "Solid Gold, Baby!" (all Golds). |

**Tips**:
- **Prep**: Buy all weapons/armor; helos/Buzzards for escape.
- **Stretch**: Stealth kill (knife) for clean.
- **Foundry**: RPGs for vehicles; switch often.
- **Max Money**: Invest Big Score earnings in Assassinations pre-ending.
