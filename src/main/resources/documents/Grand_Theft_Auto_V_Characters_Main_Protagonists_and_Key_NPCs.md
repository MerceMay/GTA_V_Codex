# Grand Theft Auto V: Characters - Main Protagonists and Key NPCs

## Overview
GTA V features **three playable protagonists** with distinct personalities, backstories, and skill trees. The story revolves around their relationships, conflicts, and interactions with a large cast of key NPCs. Below is a complete guide to the main protagonists and the most important supporting characters.

### Main Protagonists

| Name                  | Full Name                  | Age (2013) | Voiced by          | Key Traits & Background                                                                 | Special Ability                          | Skill Tree Focus                          |
|-----------------------|----------------------------|------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Franklin Clinton** | Franklin Clinton          | 25         | Shawn Fonteno      | Ambitious, street-smart repo man from South LS. Wants to escape gang life and "go legit". Loyal, quick learner, but naive at first. | **Slow Motion Driving** (Franklin slows time while driving, improves handling/aim) | Driving, Shooting, Strength, Stealth      |
| **Michael De Santa** | Michael Townley / De Santa| 48         | Ned Luke           | Retired bank robber in witness protection. Depressed, mid-life crisis, dysfunctional family. Cynical, intelligent, loves movies. | **Bullet Time** (slows time during combat, improves aim/accuracy) | Shooting, Strength, Stamina               |
| **Trevor Philips**   | Trevor Philips            | 42         | Steven Ogg         | Psychotic, volatile meth dealer/smuggler. Extremely violent, loyal to friends, hates authority. Lives in trailer in Blaine County. | **Red Mist** (increases damage resistance, melee damage, and reduces received damage) | Shooting, Strength, Flying, Driving       |

**Protagonist Dynamics**:
- Franklin: The "straight man" who bridges Michael and Trevor.
- Michael: The family man with a dark past; often the planner.
- Trevor: The chaotic wildcard; causes most conflicts but is fiercely protective.

**Character Relationships Summary**:
- **Franklin ↔ Lamar**: Best friends; brotherly bond.
- **Michael ↔ Family**: Strained but improves in Ending C.
- **Trevor ↔ Michael**: Toxic friendship from heist days; betrayal in backstory.
- **All Three ↔ Lester**: Mutual respect; heist brain.
- **Franklin ↔ Michael/Trevor**: Mentor-student dynamic with both.

**Post-Ending C (Deathwish)**: All three protagonists alive, relationships intact, full access to side content, properties, and Lester's Assassinations for maximum wealth.
