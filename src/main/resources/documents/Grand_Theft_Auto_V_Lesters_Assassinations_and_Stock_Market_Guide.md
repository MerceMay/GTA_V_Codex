# Grand Theft Auto V: Lester's Assassinations and Stock Market Guide

## Overview
Lester's Assassinations are a series of 5 optional (except the first) story missions available **only to Franklin** via phone calls at specific payphones. They teach stock manipulation for massive profits (up to ~$2.1 billion total across protagonists if timed perfectly). Each mission crashes a rival company, boosting a target stock.

**Key Rules**:
- **Order**: Do **Hotel** first (story-required, unlocks after Jewel Store Job). Save the other 4 for **AFTER The Big Score + Deathwish ending** (max cash ~$40M/person to invest).
- **Invest**: ALL 3 protagonists' money in specified stocks **BEFORE** mission (save first!). Sell at peak (sleep to advance time; Trevor sleeps 12h fastest).
- **Exchanges**: LCN (offline, player-controlled), BAWSAQ (online-simulated, slower but higher peaks).
- **Max Profit Strategy**: ~80-350% returns per mission. Total caps at $2,147,483,647 (32-bit limit); workaround by buying/selling other stocks (e.g., Ammu-Nation).
- **Post-Patch (1.14+)**: Returns reduced vs. launch (e.g., Bilkinton ~80% max).
- **Rewards**: $5k-$10k cash each + stock gains.

| Mission | Unlock Payphone | Target | Pre-Mission Buy (Peak Return) | Post-Mission Buy (Peak Return) | Total Mission Profit Potential |
|---------|-----------------|--------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| The Hotel Assassination | Lester calls (Vinewood Hills safehouse) | Brett Lowrey (Bilkinton CEO) | BettaPharma (BET, BAWSAQ, ~50%) | Bilkinton (BIL, LCN, ~80-100%+) | ~$150M+ (first, low cash) |
| The Multi Target Assassination | Little Seoul (car wash) | 4 Jurors (Redwood rivals) | Debonaire (DEB, LCN, ~50-60%) | Redwood (RWC, LCN, ~300-350%) | ~$700M+ |
| The Vice Assassination | Legion Square | Jackson Skinner (Facade CEO) | Fruit (FRT, BAWSAQ, ~25%) | Facade (FAC, BAWSAQ, ~33%) | ~$500M+ |
| The Bus Assassination | Hawick Ave | Isaac Penny (Vapid rival) | None | Vapid (VAP, BAWSAQ, ~100%) | ~$1B+ |
| The Construction Assassination | La Mesa (near LS Customs) | Enzo Bonelli (GoldCoast rival) | GoldCoast (GCD, LCN, ~45-60%) | None | ~$1.5B+ (final) |

**Total Max**: ~$2.1B if started with $40M/person post-Big Score. Do on **Monday** (sleep to set).

## General Stock Tips
- **Save Often**: Quick-save before investing/starting. Reload if stocks glitch or fail Gold.
- **Advance Time**: Sleep (Franklin 6h, Michael 8h, Trevor **12h**). Check BAWSAQ via phone/internet.
- **Sell Timing**: Monitor peaks (e.g., BET: sell after 1 sleep; BIL: 3-5 days + hold).
- **Risks**: BAWSAQ can lag (wait offline); don't spend until all done. No crew—solo Franklin.
- **Gold Medals**: Unlock achievements; higher accuracy = faster clears.
- **Replay**: Missions replayable post-story via Pause > Missions, but stocks reset—use for Gold only.

## The Hotel Assassination
**Unlock**: Automatic after Jewel Store Job setups; Lester calls Franklin (progress story to Monday).

**Brief Walkthrough**:
1. Drive to hotel rooftop (sniper spot).
2. Spot Lowrey exiting limo → **Headshot** or Sticky Bombs.
3. Lose 4-star wanted level (drive/fly away).
4. Return to Lester.

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
- Headshots: Kill with 1 headshot.
- Time: Complete within 2:00.
- Accuracy: 70%+.
- Wanted Level Efficiency: Use 4 stars.

**Stock Guide**:
| Action | Stock | Exchange | Buy Before | Sell After | Wait (In-Game Days/Hours) | Max Return |
|--------|-------|----------|------------|------------|---------------------------|------------|
| Buy | BettaPharma (BET) | BAWSAQ | Mission | Quick sell | 2-6 hours (1 sleep) | ~50% |
| Buy | Bilkinton (BIL) | LCN | After BET sell | Peak | 3 days → hold 3 more | ~80-100%+ (244% rare) |

**Tips**: First mission—low cash (~$10k invest). Sniper for Gold. Reward: $9,000.

## The Multi Target Assassination
**Unlock**: Lester calls; go to payphone (Little Seoul). Monday preferred.

**Brief Walkthrough**:
1. Gym: Kill bodybuilder (sniper/shotgun).
2. Yacht: Snipe juror.
3. Tower: Snipe window cleaner.
4. Bike: Chase/shoot cyclist in Vinewood.
5. Lose wanted level.

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
- Accuracy: 80%+.
- Time: <6:30.
- Headshots: 4+.
- Multi Kill: 2+ in 10s.

**Stock Guide**:
| Action | Stock | Exchange | Buy Before | Sell After | Wait | Max Return |
|--------|-------|----------|------------|------------|------|------------|
| Buy | Debonaire (DEB) | LCN | Mission | Peak | 2 days | ~50-60% |
| Buy | Redwood (RWC) | LCN | After DEB | Peak | 4-6 days | ~300-350%+ |

**Tips**: Sniper all for speed. Drive fast between. Huge payout—reinvest. Reward: $7,000.

## The Vice Assassination
**Unlock**: Lester calls; Legion Square payphone.

**Brief Walkthrough**:
1. Park near strip club.
2. Wait for Skinner's limo (after decoys).
3. Snipe driver or bomb car → escape (no stars = bonus).

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
- Time: <2:00.
- Accuracy: 100%.
- No Wanted Level: Escape clean.
- Headshots: 1.

**Stock Guide**:
| Action | Stock | Exchange | Buy Before | Sell After | Wait | Max Return |
|--------|-------|----------|------------|------------|------|------------|
| Buy | Fruit (FRT) | BAWSAQ | Mission | Quick | 6-8 hours | ~25% |
| Buy | Facade (FAC) | BAWSAQ | After FRT | Peak | 2 days | ~33% |

**Tips**: Snipe from afar. No sleep needed for FRT. Reward: $3,000 (+$2k clean).

## The Bus Assassination
**Unlock**: Hawick Ave payphone.

**Brief Walkthrough**:
1. Board bus → ride route.
2. Confront Penny at stop → chase/kill (bus run-over bonus).
3. Escape.

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
- Time: <2:00.
- Bus Kill: Run over with bus.
- Accuracy: N/A.
- Unmarked: No damage to bus.

**Stock Guide**:
| Action | Stock | Exchange | Buy Before | Sell After | Wait | Max Return |
|--------|-------|----------|------------|------------|------|------------|
| Buy | Vapid (VAP) | BAWSAQ | After mission | Peak | 2 days | ~100% |

**Tips**: Bus kill for bonus/Gold. Massive single-play profit. Reward: $5,000 (+$2k bus).

## The Construction Assassination
**Unlock**: La Mesa payphone (final).

**Brief Walkthrough**:
1. Drive to site → sniper guards/rooftop.
2. Shoot chopper → parachute down.
3. Call Lester → end.

**Gold Medal Checklist**:
- Time: <4:30.
- Accuracy: 80%+.
- Headshots: 7+.
- Parachute: Land in landing zone.

**Stock Guide**:
| Action | Stock | Exchange | Buy Before | Sell After | Wait | Max Return |
|--------|-------|----------|------------|------------|------|------------|
| Buy | GoldCoast (GCD) | LCN | Mission | Peak | 1-2 days | ~45-60% |

**Tips**: Heavy sniper for chopper. Final—spend after! Reward: $10,000.

**Final Notes**: Total ~$2.1B enables property buys, Online prep. Replay for Gold via menu (no stock gains).
