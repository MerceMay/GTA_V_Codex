# Grand Theft Auto V: Heists - Plans, Setups, and Choices

## Overview
GTA V features five major story mode Heists planned by Lester Crest, unlocked progressively after specific missions. They involve Michael, Franklin, and Trevor as protagonists, with optional hired crew for some. Key elements include **Setups** (preparation missions), **Approaches/Plans** (two options per heist where applicable), **Crew Choices** (affect cuts, performance, and risks), and **Payouts** (maximized by optimal crew/experience to avoid losses like dropped loot or casualties).

| Heist | Unlocks After | Protagonists | Approaches | Max Gross Take | Notes |
|-------|---------------|--------------|------------|----------------|-------|
| The Jewel Store Job | Casing the Jewel Store | Michael (leader), Franklin | Smart / Loud | $4,946,153 | First heist; crew hires unlocked |
| The Merryweather Heist | Nervous Ron | Trevor (leader), Michael, Franklin | Freighter / Offshore | $0 | No cash; story device retrieval |
| Blitz Play | Boiler Suits (setup) | Michael, Franklin, Trevor | Single (fixed) | Bonds (no direct cash) | FIB-mandated; setups only |
| The Bureau Raid | The Bureau Raid planning | Michael (leader), Franklin | Fire Crew / Roof | $331,985 | FIB cover-up; Franklin's cut highest |
| The Big Score | Gauntlets (setup) | Michael (leader), Franklin, Trevor | Subtle / Obvious | $201,600,000 | Final heist; highest payout |

**General Tips**:
- Crew skills improve after surviving heists (Poor → Mediocre → Good).
- Cheap crew (low % cut) + experience = max profit (avoid losses > cuts).
- Gold Medals unlock achievements/trophies.

## The Jewel Store Job
**Overview**: First heist. Smash 20 displays for max take. Smart (gas knockout) is canon/easier.

**Setups** (mandatory):
- Bugstars Equipment: Steal Burrito van.
- BZ Gas Grenades: Steal gas van.
- Carbine Rifles: Steal from LSPD van.

**Approaches**:

| Approach | Description | Crew Reco (Total Cut) | Max Net (after cuts/Madrazo payoff) |
|----------|-------------|-----------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Smart** | Gas via vent; ransack silently | Driver: Karim (8%), Gunman: Packie (12%), Hacker: Rickie (4%) → 24% | Michael: ~$1.22M |
| **Loud** | Hostages; smash cases | Driver: Eddie (14%), Gunman: Packie (12%), Hacker: Rickie (4%) → 30% | Similar; poor gunman fails control |

**Payout**: Gross $4.95M → Net ~$2.45M (after $2.5M Madrazo). Poor driver/gunman drops ~10% loot.
**Tips**: Smart for Gold (50s alarm via Rickie); smash all cases; pick up drops.

## The Merryweather Heist
**Overview**: Steal superweapon prototype (returned, no cash). Trevor leads.

**Setups**:
- **Both**: Minisub (steal Kuruma).
- **Offshore only**: Cargobob (steal helicopter).

**Approaches**:

| Approach | Description | Differences |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Freighter** | Sniper cover; plant bombs on ship; dive for device | Easier Gold (headshots); Trevor retrieves |
| **Offshore** | Cargobob drops sub; fight Merryweather | Flight School helps; harder Gold (time/weather) |

**Payout**: $0 (Lester returns device).
**Tips**: Freighter for easier Gold; story-only.

## Blitz Play
**Overview**: Rob IAA armored truck for FIB. Fixed plan, no crew hires.

**Setups**:
- Masks (Vespucci Masks).
- Boiler Suits (Ammu-Nation).
- Trash Truck (East Vinewood).
- Tow Truck (La Puerta).
- Getaway Vehicle (any fast car).

**Approach**: Single - Tow truck rams; block road; shootout vs. LSPD/NOOSE.
**Payout**: Bearer Bonds (to Devin Weston; no direct cash).
**Tips**: Gold - 12 headshots, 60% accuracy, down helicopter (Trevor RPG), switch chars 10x.

## The Bureau Raid
**Overview**: Steal data from FIB HQ. Michael leads; Franklin gets main cut.

**Setups** (Fire Crew only):
- Fire Truck.
- Getaway Vehicle.

**Approaches**:

| Approach | Description | Crew Reco (Total Cut) | Franklin's Max Cut |
|----------|-------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| **Fire Crew (Covert)** | Fake fire; plant bombs as janitor | Gunman x2: Daryl (6%) + Hugh (7%) → 16.5% (+casualty?) | ~$240k |
| **Roof (Loud)** | Parachute roof entry; hack/defend | Driver: Taliana (5%), Gunman: Daryl (6%), Hacker: Rickie (4%) → 15% | $242,349 |

**Payout**: Gross $332k → Lester 12%, Crew 15%, Franklin bulk.
**Tips**: Roof for max/easier (ambulance escape); 20 headshots, 70% accuracy.

## The Big Score
**Overview**: Steal $201M gold from Union Depository. Finale; Obvious = max profit.

**Setups**:
- **Subtle**: Police Van, Gauntlets (x3, modded).
- **Obvious**: Cutter, Freight Train (Skylift), Getaway Vehicle.

**Approaches**:

| Approach | Description | Crew Reco (Total Cut) | Each Protagonist Max |
|----------|-------------|-----------------------|----------------------|
| **Subtle** | Spike tires; traffic hack; Gauntlet chase | 4 drivers/gunmen + Hacker → 35% + $300k bribe | ~$35.5M |
| **Obvious** | Drill vault; helicopter/train escape | 2 drivers/pilots + 2 gunmen → 26% | ~$41.7M |

**Payout**: Gross $201.6M. Obvious nets ~$6M more per person (smaller crew).
**Tips**: Obvious + Taliana (Driver 1) + Karim (2) + cheap gunmen; avoid crashes (50% loss); Gold - 20 headshots, <16min (Obvious). Choose "Deathwish" ending for full shares.
