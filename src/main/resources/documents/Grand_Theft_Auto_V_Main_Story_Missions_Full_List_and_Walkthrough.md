# Grand Theft Auto V: Main Story Missions - Full List and Walkthrough

## Overview
GTA V's main storyline consists of **69 missions** across three protagonists: **Franklin Clinton (F)**, **Michael De Santa (M)**, and **Trevor Philips (T)**. Missions unlock progressively, often with setups for heists. Total playtime: ~30-50 hours. 100% completion requires all 69 + other content.

**Key Tips**:
- Switch protagonists via map/phone (unlocks gradually).
- Gold Medals: Earn via checklists (accuracy, headshots, time, etc.) for achievements/trophies.
- Heists: Choose approaches (Smart/Loud) for variety/payouts.
- Save before choices/endings (3 finales: A/B/C; C = Deathwish = canon/max payout).
- Payouts: Cumulative ~$200M+ by end (invest via stocks/Lester's Assassinations).

## Detailed Walkthroughs by Chapter

### Prologue (Ludendorff, North Yankton)
**Mission 1: Prologue**  
**Steps**: Play as Michael/Trevor/Brad in 9-year flashback bank heist. Shoot guards, grab cash, escape in snowmobile/car, betrayals unfold.  
**Gold**: Cutscene/tutorial - no req.  
**Tips**: Follow prompts; story intro to trio dynamic.  
**Payout**: None (sets plot).

### Los Santos Introduction (Simeon Yetarian / Early Michael/Franklin)
**Mission 2: Franklin and Lamar**  
**Steps**: 1. Repo Bravado Buffalo S & Obey & Fugitive. 2. Race Lamar through LS streets.  
**Gold Checklist**: - Time: <9:00 total. - Not a Scratch: No major damage. - Focused Driver: 8 drifts/ramps.  
**Tips**: Win races for bonus; use Franklin slow-mo. **Payout**: $250.
